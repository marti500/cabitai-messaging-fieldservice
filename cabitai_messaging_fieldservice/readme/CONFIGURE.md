In Field Service, Configuration, Settings, section WhatsApp:

1. Choose the WhatsApp account and the language of the templates.
2. Tick the reminder (and its hours before the visit) and/or the completion message.
3. Choose what happens when the customer answers: the stage when they confirm
   (optional), whether Cancel cancels the order or creates an activity, and who gets
   the activities.
4. Click **Submit templates to Meta**. The status of each template is shown next to
   it; reminders start as soon as Meta approves them.
